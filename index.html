I am an astronomy PhD student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, based at the National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences (NAOC), working with Prof. Chao Liu and Prof. Juanjuan Ren. I study the structure of nearby star-forming regions using three-dimensional dust maps and methods from complexity science.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

From cloud structure to stellar birth masses.

My broader interest is the physical origin of the core mass function and the stellar initial mass function. I am interested in connecting cloud geometry with gas dynamics, physical conditions, and fragmentation.

  • Molecular clouds
  • Star formation
  • Fractal structure
  • 3D dust maps
